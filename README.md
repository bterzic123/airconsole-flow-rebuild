# Growth Audit — AirConsole

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/airconsole-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic AirConsole already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and Hero paywall rebuilt in AirConsole's own design. Only the sequence changes. The art direction stays as it is.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show their game night before the price: ask how many are playing, then show that the free Starter Pack stops at 2 players | CR +10–20% |
| 02 | Name their group and screen on the paywall, with your 4.7 from 5.1K App Store ratings | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Offer the $29.99 lifetime Hero once, when someone closes the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask for tracking after the paywall, not on the welcome carousel | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Start on the annual plan your 70% OFF label already points at | ARPU +3–8% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for AirConsole. Every price, rating and feature line comes from AirConsole's own App Store listing, website, pricing page and in-app screens.

python3 -m http.server 8921
